## Overview of Reviewer Feedback Classification

We cataloged and analyzed all reviewer criticisms, methodological questions, and post-review audit items across the HealthScore manuscript lifecycle (`old paper.pdf` $\to$ `final_paper.pdf` $\to$ `final_crc_paper.pdf`), classifying every item into the six required domain categories:

1. **Category 1:** Classification threshold and precision–recall trade-off (`R1.1` – `R1.4`)
2. **Category 2:** Model selection, baseline comparisons, and evaluation methodology (`R2.1` – `R2.5`)
3. **Category 3:** Data preprocessing, survey weighting, and target construction (`R3.1` – `R3.4`)
4. **Category 4:** Blockchain performance, architecture, or simulation claims (`R4.1` – `R4.4`)
5. **Category 5:** Actuarial / insurance modeling claims (`R5.1` – `R5.2`)
6. **Category 6:** Writing clarity, structure, citations, tables, figures, and presentation (`R6.1` – `R6.3`)
